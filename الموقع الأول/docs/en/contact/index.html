FAQ

Before you order

How do I request a quote?

Send us the product, quantity and size on WhatsApp and we will reply with a clear quote during working hours.

Do you design, or do I send a ready file?

Both. We design with you from the idea, or print your file as it is after checking it for print.

Which file format is best for print?

A high-resolution PDF in CMYK with a 3 mm bleed, or the open design file.

Do you show a proof before the full run?

Yes, we send you a proof to approve before producing the quantity.

Where are you?

In Dammam, and we deliver across Dammam and the Eastern Province.
